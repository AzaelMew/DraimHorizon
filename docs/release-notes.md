**DraimHorizon 0.40.4 — QOL tab, Auto Tools held-tool trigger and Auto Eat "Eat to heal"**

Target: official Horizon Nightly source `9f42064c11efe7ef3e5ed2baad7ddc2a3b36eea0`.

- New optional **Trigger only when holding tool** toggle under Auto Tools (J → QOL → Gear; Russian: «Только с инструментом в руке»). It is off by default, so existing setups behave as before, and it does not turn Auto Tools on by itself.
- When on, Auto Tools switches hotbar slots only while your main hand holds a pickaxe, axe or shovel. Swords, food, hoes, shears, an empty hand and off-hand tools do not trigger it, and switching stops immediately if you select one of them mid-attack.
- Only the item category is checked: the held tool need not suit the block or be fresh. Automated mining, construction, farming and durability protection are unchanged.

- New optional **Eat to heal** toggle under Auto Eat (J → QOL → Survival; Russian: «Есть для лечения»), off by default. With Auto Eat on and your health below your actual maximum, an allowed safe food may be eaten even if it overfills hunger: a food that fits is still preferred, otherwise the smallest one is used. It never eats at full hunger and does nothing at full health, with the toggle off, or with invalid health.
- This only lets Auto Eat waste hunger while you are injured. It is not a custom HP target, not instant healing, and does not touch health directly: recovery still comes from the server's normal regeneration. Allowed-food list, risky-food exclusions, modified food components, interruption by attacks/use and farm supply checks are unchanged.

- Auto Tools now looks at the hotbar only by default. A new **Allow from inventory** toggle (J → QOL → Gear, beside the held-tool toggle; Russian: «Разрешить из инвентаря») also lets it take a tool from your inventory, swapping it into the hotbar the normal way (rate limited, one swap at a time). Off by default.
- Fixed: a fresher tool in the inventory could win the ranking over a usable worn hotbar tool and then be discarded, so nothing was switched. The search is now limited to the allowed slots before ranking. The held-tool condition still stops all automatic switching and inventory moves when you hold a sword, food or nothing. Automated mining, construction and farming tool selection is unchanged.

- **Helpers has its own tab, called QOL in English** (Russian name unchanged: «Помощники»), in the J menu between Trading and Experiments, grouped by task: **Survival** (Auto Eat, Allowed foods, Eat to heal, Auto Totem, Protect glow berry vines, Show invisible entities), **Gear** (Auto Fill, Auto Tools and its two options, Shulker preview) and **Automation** (Auto Resume, Human Look, Auto Click with Button and Rate, Auto reconnect). Those four extra switches moved here from Settings → General; nothing about how they work changed. Each switch sits with the choices that depend on it; those choices are dimmed and say what they need while the switch is off. Experiments now holds only Ores and Baritone.
- Keyboard: Tab / Shift+Tab switch category, Page Up / Page Down turn pages, Escape leaves the food picker. Settings, keys and defaults are unchanged, and switching a helper on before accepting the Experiments warning still shows it.
- Every on/off option in QOL is a switch with an on/off word. Show invisible entities still asks for the experimental warning before it can be turned on and is labelled with its ban risk.
