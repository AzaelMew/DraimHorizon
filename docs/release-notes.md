**DraimHorizon 0.39.11 - Farm plots**

Target: official Horizon Nightly source `fdc87aecdbd116bf1e08adcd062fcbaea8c2e2ca`.

- Mark fields with `/farm pos1`, `/farm pos2` and `/farm save [name]`; the selection is drawn in yellow, `/farm list` and `/farm clear` help.
- The Farm tab lists the plots of the world: farm on/off, show/hide, delete.
- The farm works the enabled plots of the world wherever it starts, so it finds its field again after a reconnect or a restart.
- No harvest limit: the farm runs until it is stopped.
- Schematic previews are found again after a restart.

Native client compiled against the pinned Nightly. Not verified in game.
