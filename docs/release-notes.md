**DraimHorizon 0.40.5 — QOL tab, Auto Tools and Auto Eat options, bounded reply hiding, claims fix**

Target: official Horizon Nightly source `9f42064c11efe7ef3e5ed2baad7ddc2a3b36eea0`.

- P2 review corrections: repeated indistinguishable manual economy requests, or a manual request behind an identical automatic one, keep automatic economy checks paused through the existing ten-second wait. A quote, item-limit and Moves line can be parts of ONE worth response, not three completed requests. These lines cannot release overlapping waits early; observed matching replies still get the existing trailing three-second grace. The four-entry/overflow bound, two-message automatic budget and four-second expiry are unchanged.
- A manual `/bal`, `/balance` or `/worth` typed before the first economy frame now keeps its protection when the scope loads, even if chat arrives before loading. Attribution checks the current remote server/profile at command/chat hooks too, rather than trusting the previous frame's identity. Same-scope frames keep pending protection; changing server/account, disconnecting or switching the mod off drops the old session state, and disabling Economy clears its reply attribution. No scanner behavior, claim-info preference, map persistence/sharing or helper defaults changed.
- This remains bounded, best-effort attribution without server request IDs: identical late replies beyond the wait/grace can still be mistaken for automatic output. The new changes have regression/native tests, not fresh live-server acceptance.
- New optional **Trigger only when holding tool** toggle under Auto Tools (J → QOL → Gear; Russian: «Только с инструментом в руке»). It is off by default, so existing setups behave as before, and it does not turn Auto Tools on by itself.
- When on, Auto Tools switches hotbar slots only while your main hand holds a pickaxe, axe or shovel. Swords, food, hoes, shears, an empty hand and off-hand tools do not trigger it, and switching stops immediately if you select one of them mid-attack.
- Only the item category is checked: the held tool need not suit the block or be fresh. Automated mining, construction, farming and durability protection are unchanged.

- New optional **Eat to heal** toggle under Auto Eat (J → QOL → Survival; Russian: «Есть для лечения»), off by default. With Auto Eat on and your health below your actual maximum, an allowed safe food may be eaten even if it overfills hunger: a food that fits is still preferred, otherwise the smallest one is used. It never eats at full hunger and does nothing at full health, with the toggle off, or with invalid health.
- This only lets Auto Eat waste hunger while you are injured. It is not a custom HP target, not instant healing, and does not touch health directly: recovery still comes from the server's normal regeneration. Allowed-food list, risky-food exclusions, modified food components, interruption by attacks/use and farm supply checks are unchanged.

- Auto Tools now looks at the hotbar only by default. A new **Allow from inventory** toggle (J → QOL → Gear, beside the held-tool toggle; Russian: «Разрешить из инвентаря») also lets it take a tool from your inventory, swapping it into the hotbar the normal way (rate limited, one swap at a time). Off by default.
- Fixed: a fresher tool in the inventory could win the ranking over a usable worn hotbar tool and then be discarded, so nothing was switched. The search is now limited to the allowed slots before ranking. The held-tool condition still stops all automatic switching and inventory moves when you hold a sword, food or nothing. Automated mining, construction and farming tool selection is unchanged.

- **Helpers has its own tab, called QOL in English** (Russian name unchanged: «Помощники»), in the J menu between Trading and Experiments, grouped by task: **Survival** (Auto Eat, Allowed foods, Eat to heal, Auto Totem, Protect glow berry vines, Show invisible entities), **Gear** (Auto Fill, Auto Tools and its two options, Shulker preview) and **Controls** (Auto Resume, Human Look, Auto Click with Button and Rate, Auto reconnect). Protect glow berry vines, Show invisible entities, Shulker preview and Auto reconnect moved here from Settings → General; nothing about how they work changed. Russian group names: Выживание, Снаряжение, Управление. Each switch sits with the choices that depend on it; those choices are dimmed and say what they need while the switch is off. Experiments → Automation (Farm and Drain), the Baritone tab and the Schematics tab are unchanged and keep their own place; the QOL group named Controls is separate from them, and no Farm, Drain or Baritone control is in QOL.
- Keyboard: Tab / Shift+Tab switch category, Page Up / Page Down turn pages, Escape leaves the food picker. Settings, keys and defaults are unchanged, and switching a helper on before accepting the Experiments warning still shows it.
- Every on/off option in QOL is a switch with an on/off word. Show invisible entities still asks for the experimental warning before it can be turned on and is labelled with its ban risk.

- Fixed: Auto Eat is on by default but now only eats once you have accepted the experimental warning (it used to be reachable only behind that warning). You can still choose foods beforehand and switch it off; switching it on asks for the warning.
- Fixed: Page Up / Page Down now page the QOL tab and the food picker (they were ignored), and keypad Enter now saves in the translation editor.
- Fixed: Eat to heal no longer treats a full health bar as injured when your maximum health is fractional (for example 20.3).

- "Hide routine command replies" now hides only replies to checks the mod runs by itself: after an automatic `/worth` or `/bal`, only its own recognized output in the next two incoming messages is hidden. A `/worth`, `/bal` or `/balance` you type cancels the window and pauses automatic checks for a moment, so its reply is not hidden as automatic (best effort: the server gives replies no request id, so a reply arriving in the same moment can still be misjudged); anything unrecognized stays visible. Two messages is an assumption, not a verified server rule, so a longer `/worth` answer may show its later lines.

- Fixed: with "Hide routine command replies" on, a `/claims` you typed showed no list. Only the list from the mod's own background refresh when you join a server can be hidden now (within the next two incoming messages), and a typed `/claims` cancels that. Refreshes you start yourself show their list, and your claims and the map update either way.

- Built on the published 0.40.4 (Baritone navigation port, Schematics tab and material list, global chat switch, minimised-window support), integrated rather than replaced: the J menu now has nine destinations above Settings, so the side rail needs a 302-high panel and shorter panels use the compact menu, whose rows shrink to fit. A menu view saved by 0.40.4 under Experiments → Helpers opens the QOL tab.

- Command replies the mod hides, checked one by one. **/claim info** is never run by the mod, so its reply is now shown; a new switch **Hide /claim info replies** (Russian: «Скрывать ответ /claim info») in Map and claims → Storage, off by default, hides it for those who want that and applies at once. For **/trust**, **/claims** and **/worth, /bal, /balance** the mod hides only the replies it can attribute to its own automatic sends (in the next two incoming messages, when "Hide routine command replies" is on). What you type is sent as written. A command you type cancels the matching window and keeps the mod from running its own such command until the answer to yours has been seen (at most ten seconds); unrelated balance, quote or buy/sell lines do not end that wait. The short grace for the rest of a multi-line answer starts when your reply is seen, and if you type more such commands at once than can be tracked, the wait is held to its deadline.
- Limits, stated plainly: the server gives replies no request id, so this is best effort and not a guarantee. If your reply takes longer than the ten-second wait, or arrives after the mod has already sent its own identical query, an identical late line can still be taken for the mod's and hidden. Where the code can tell a reply is ambiguous it is left visible. Two messages is an assumption, not a verified rule. Nothing was tested against a live server.
