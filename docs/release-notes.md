**DraimHorizon 0.37.7 - Waypoints, pop-ups and coordinates**

Target: official Horizon Nightly source `fdc87aecdbd116bf1e08adcd062fcbaea8c2e2ca`.

- The waypoints tab no longer slides under the rail.
- A new waypoint editor and quick waypoint pop-up with captions, ringed colour swatches and readable X, Y, Z in whole blocks; the Appearance pop-up shares the new frame.
- On/off buttons are drawn as switches everywhere the shared button is used.
- The coordinates under the minimap are easier to read; in the Nether the overworld position is shown too.
- Builds on 0.37.6: the shulker tooltip without the list.
